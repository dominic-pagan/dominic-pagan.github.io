What is Qilo?

Qilo is a technology startup leveraging blockchain technology that offers a combined social media and online learning platform.

Qilo addresses the student loan debt crisis by giving students free access to online courses and certifications, as well as allowing students to earn money by completing cryptocurrency company specific courses. Through utilizing blockchain technology, I devised and implemented a novel closed loop economic model that allows users to engage, earn, and learn.
