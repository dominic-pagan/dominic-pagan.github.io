What is Cura AI?

Cura AI is an award winning technology startup that leverages deep learning technology to scan chest x-rays for ailments such as pneumonia, tuberculosis, emphysema, and fibrosis.

Cura AI addresses the global shortage of radiologists by giving patients access to medical image analysis using image recognition. Using machine learning, Cura parses through medical imaging data, learns how to identify an ailment in the images, and then determines the presence of the ailment.

What is the University of Washington Dempsey Startup Competition?

The Dempsey Startup Competition is a regional university startup business plan competition where undergrads and grad students at accredited colleges and universities across the Cascadia Corridor – Washington, Oregon, Idaho, and British Columbia, as well as Alaska - compete in a series of rounds judged by hundreds of prominent figures in the Northwest entrepreneurial ecosystem. Participating in the Dempsey Startup Competition allowed me to gain real world experience in the dynamics of venture creation by developing an idea, putting together a founding team, writing a business plan, and presenting to investors.
